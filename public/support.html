Support

Peach of a Word is a daily word game. You get eight letters and find the words they spell. One of those words uses all eight: the peach of a word the whole board grew from. You can play it here at peachofaword.com or in the iPhone app.

One New Board a Day

A new board arrives at midnight where you are, and everyone gets the same board for that date.

Your Progress Stays on Your Device

There is no account and no sign-in. Your found words and your streak are stored on the device you play on: inside the app, or in this browser. The app and the website keep their own progress separately. Deleting the app or clearing your browser storage clears it, and there is no copy on a server to restore it from.

The privacy page says exactly what the app and the website collect.
