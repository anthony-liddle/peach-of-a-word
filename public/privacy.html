Privacy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Peach of a Word has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no server that knows anything about you. This page covers two things: the iOS app and the website at peachofaword.com. They are not identical, so they are described separately.

The iOS app collects nothing, and makes no network calls

That second half is the part worth stating plainly, because it is what makes the first half verifiable rather than a promise. The app does not contact any server, ours or anyone else's. There is no analytics library, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party code of any kind in it.

  • The dictionary and the puzzles ship inside the app. Nothing is fetched while you play, and the app works with the network switched off.
  • Your found words, your streak and your settings are stored on your own device. They are never sent anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • Tapping share hands a spoiler-free summary to iOS, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is shared unless you do that, and it goes wherever you send it rather than to us.

The website collects aggregate page views, and nothing else

The game itself works the same way as the app: the puzzle data ships with the page, and your progress, theme and text size live in your browser's local storage under the keys eight-letters/v1, e8-theme and e8-text-size. Clearing your browser storage clears them. There is no account and no copy of them anywhere else.

The one thing the website does that the app does not is count visits. It uses Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookieless and aggregate. Per Vercel's documentation it records the time, the page URL, the referring site, an approximate location from your network connection, and your device type, operating system and browser. Visitors are identified by a hash derived from the request rather than by a cookie, and that is discarded after 24 hours. It does not follow you to other sites and it does not build a profile of you.

The site is hosted on Vercel, which like any web host processes the requests needed to serve pages.

What is never collected, on either

  • No names, email addresses, or accounts, because there are none.
  • No contacts, photos, location permissions, or device identifiers.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, and no data sold or shared.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing that could be used to identify you personally.

Children

The game is suitable for children and collects nothing from anyone, which means there is nothing held about a child to request or delete.

Changes, and how to ask
