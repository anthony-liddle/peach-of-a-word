Privacy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Peach of a Word has no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising. There is no server anywhere holding anything about you. Two versions exist, the iOS app and the website at peachofaword.com, and they do not behave the same way, so this page takes them one at a time.

The iOS App Makes No Network Calls

Not to a server of ours, and not to anyone else's. There is no analytics library in it, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party code of any kind.

  • The dictionary and the puzzles ship inside the app. Nothing is fetched while you play, and the app works with the network switched off.
  • Your found words, your streak and your settings are stored on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Tapping share hands a spoiler-free summary to iOS, and you pick where it goes.

The Website Counts Visits

The game works the way the app does. Puzzle data ships with the page, and your progress, theme and text size live in your browser's local storage under the keys eight-letters/v1, e8-theme and e8-text-size. Clearing your browser storage clears them. There is no account and no copy of them anywhere else.

Counting visits is the one thing the site does that the app does not. It uses Vercel Web Analytics, cookieless and aggregate. Per Vercel's documentation it records the time, the page URL, the referring site, an approximate location from your network connection, and your device type, operating system and browser. Visitors are identified by a hash derived from the request, not by a cookie, and it is discarded after 24 hours. It does not follow you to other sites, and it does not build a profile of you.

Vercel hosts the site, and like any web host it processes the requests needed to serve pages.

What Neither One Collects

No accounts exist, so there are no names or email addresses to hold. Neither version asks for your contacts, your photos, your location, or a device identifier. Nothing is sold or shared, and no ad network is involved. Nothing here follows you into another app or another website, and neither version holds anything that could identify you personally.

Children

Nothing is collected from anyone, children included, so there is nothing held about a child to ask about or to have deleted.

Changes and Questions
